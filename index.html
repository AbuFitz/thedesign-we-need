How we buy

A straightforward process before any product is listed for sale.

1

Review product data and case quantities

We check specifications, packaging and case sizes against what marketplace listings require.

2

Check marketplace eligibility and compliance

We confirm the product and brand can be listed, and that any required certification is in place.

3

Place a sensible test order

New lines start with a modest first order to confirm quality, packaging and delivery before scaling up.
