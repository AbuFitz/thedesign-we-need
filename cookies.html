Cookies

This site doesn't use cookies for analytics, advertising, or tracking.

What this site stores, and where

This site does not set any cookies. The only thing it stores in your browser is your shopping cart contents, held in localStorage (not a cookie) on your own device so it's still there if you come back. It is never sent to us or anyone else automatically — it only leaves your browser when you actually start checkout, and only the SKU/quantity data needed to do that.

No analytics, no advertising

There is no Google Analytics, no advertising pixel, and no third-party tracking script anywhere on this site.

When Stripe is involved

If and when checkout is actually open for a product, starting checkout hands you off to Stripe's own hosted payment page, which is outside this site and governed by Stripe's own privacy and cookie policy. We don't control what Stripe's pages do.
