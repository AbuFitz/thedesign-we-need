Process

How we buy

A consistent process applied to every new product before it's added to a listing, and before any order is placed.

1

Review product data and case quantities

We look at the product specification, packaging, dimensions and case or carton quantities, and check them against what a marketplace listing actually needs.

2

Check marketplace eligibility and compliance

We confirm the product category, brand and any required certification are accepted for the marketplaces we sell through, including Amazon UK, before agreeing anything further.

3

Place a sensible test order

For a new supplier or a new line, we start with a modest first order. This lets us check quality, packaging and delivery reliability before committing to larger volumes.

4

Reorder products that perform reliably

Where a product sells consistently and the supply relationship works well on both sides, we reorder and look to build a longer-term arrangement.
