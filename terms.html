About this website

This website is operated by A. Sharif Commerce, an independent UK sole trader based in Hemel Hempstead, Hertfordshire. The site previews a product catalogue and introduces the business to prospective customers and suppliers. It does not currently process sales or take payments — every product is in preview and checkout is gated closed; see product lifecycle status.

When checkout does open

Once a product is marked checkout-ready and payment can actually be taken, a contract of sale is formed when payment is confirmed by Stripe, not when an order is placed. Nothing on a product page before that point is a binding offer to sell at the price shown — prices shown as "to be confirmed" are exactly that, not a quote.
