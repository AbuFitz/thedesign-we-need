What we source

A small, deliberately narrow set of categories, chosen for steady demand and straightforward compliance rather than breadth.

Stationery & office essentials

Pens, notebooks, filing and desk consumables — everyday reorder items with predictable demand and simple listings.

Home & kitchen

Practical kitchenware, storage and household items where quality and consistent packaging matter for repeat orders.

Cleaning & household

Recognised brands and everyday consumables, with clear ingredient and safety labelling for marketplace compliance.

Pet essentials

Everyday pet care and accessory lines from suppliers who can provide clear product and safety documentation.

Toys, gifts & party

Seasonal and everyday lines that meet UK toy safety and marketplace listing requirements.

Selected health & beauty

We only take on health and beauty lines where documentation and marketplace requirements are clear and verifiable in advance.

We deliberately keep this list narrow. If your range sits outside these categories but you think it could still be a fit, get in touch and we'll say plainly whether it's something we're able to look at.
