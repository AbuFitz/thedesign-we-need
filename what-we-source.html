What we source

A small, deliberately narrow set of categories, chosen for steady demand and straightforward compliance rather than breadth — the same categories you'll see in the shop itself.

  1. 01

    Cable management

    Clips and organiser boxes for desk and TV cables — everyday reorder items with predictable demand and simple listings.

  2. 02

    Travel & packing

    Packing cubes and similar organisers, where consistent sizing and packaging matter for repeat orders.

  3. 03

    Home storage

    Drawer dividers, hanging wardrobe organisers and similar practical storage items.

  4. 04

    Kitchen

    Sink racks, sink caddies and similar — food-adjacent items need supplier documentation on material safety before we list them.

  5. 05

    Cleaning & utility

    Wall-mounted holders and similar utility items — wall-fixed products need confirmed load/fixing information before we list them.

We deliberately keep this list narrow.
