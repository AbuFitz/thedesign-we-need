This page is a template. It gives a reasonable starting point for a small UK e-commerce site, but it is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a solicitor. Abukar Sharif should review it, and have it checked by a qualified professional, before checkout actually opens for any product.

Who this notice covers

This notice applies to visitors to the A. Sharif Commerce website, including the shop, product pages and cart. It does not cover any separate marketplace platform (such as Amazon UK) that A. Sharif Commerce also sells through — those platforms have their own privacy notices — nor Stripe's own payment pages, which are covered by Stripe's privacy policy.

What information is collected

This website does not use non-essential cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools. No personal data is automatically collected from visitors browsing the site.

Your cart contents are stored only in your own browser (localStorage), not sent to us until you start checkout — see Cookies for detail.

If you contact us by email, the information you choose to include (name, email address, anything else you write) is received and kept to respond to your enquiry.

If and when checkout is open and you place an order, payment details are entered directly on Stripe's hosted page — we never see or store your card details. We do receive your order contents, delivery details and email address from Stripe to fulfil the order.

How that information is used

  • To respond to customer and supplier enquiries.
  • To process and fulfil orders, once checkout is open.
  • To keep a record of ongoing supplier conversations and agreements.

Information is not sold, and is not shared with third parties except where required to fulfil an order or supply arrangement, or by law.

How long information is kept

Email correspondence and order records are kept for as long as reasonably needed for business, tax, and record-keeping purposes, and deleted when no longer needed.

Your rights

If you've contacted us or placed an order and want to know what information is held about you, or want it corrected or deleted, email a.sharifcommerce@outlook.com.

Changes to this notice

This notice will be updated as the business develops, in particular once checkout actually opens for the first time.
