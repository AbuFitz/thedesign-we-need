Who this notice covers

This notice applies to visitors to the A. Sharif Commerce website. It does not cover any separate marketplace platform (such as Amazon UK) that A. Sharif Commerce sells through — those platforms have their own privacy notices.

What information is collected

This website does not use non-essential cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools. No personal data is automatically collected from visitors browsing the site.

If you contact A. Sharif Commerce by email, the information you choose to include in that email (such as your name, company name and email address) is received and kept for the purpose of responding to your enquiry.

How that information is used

  • To respond to enquiries from prospective suppliers, distributors and other correspondents.
  • To keep a record of ongoing supplier conversations and agreements.

Information is not sold, and is not shared with third parties except where required to fulfil a supply arrangement or by law.

How long information is kept

Email correspondence is kept for as long as reasonably needed for business and record-keeping purposes, and is deleted when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

If you have contacted A. Sharif Commerce and would like to know what information is held about you, or would like it corrected or deleted, email a.sharifcommerce@outlook.com.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as the business develops. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
