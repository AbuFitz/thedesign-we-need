About A. Sharif Commerce

An independent, early-stage business built one supplier relationship and one product line at a time.

Where we are today

A. Sharif Commerce is an independent UK sole-trader e-commerce business, run by Abukar Sharif and based in Hemel Hempstead, Hertfordshire. We buy practical, everyday products from established wholesalers and sell them through trusted online marketplaces, including Amazon UK.

This is a deliberately early-stage business. We're not a large importer or a distribution company, and we don't claim to be — we're building supplier relationships gradually, starting with a narrow range of categories and testing new lines carefully before scaling them up.

What we can offer a supplier is straightforward: a genuine buyer, clear requirements, honest communication, and orders paid for properly against a real invoice. This page exists so a prospective supplier can check who they'd be dealing with before we speak.
