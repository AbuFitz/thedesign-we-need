About A. Sharif Commerce

An independent, early-stage business, built one supplier relationship and one honest product listing at a time.

Where we are today

A. Sharif Commerce is an independent UK sole-trader e-commerce business, run by Abukar Sharif and based in Hemel Hempstead, Hertfordshire. We buy practical, everyday products from established wholesalers and sell them through trusted online marketplaces, including Amazon UK — and we're building this website as our own direct storefront alongside that.

This is a deliberately early-stage business. We're not a large retailer and we don't claim to be — the catalogue you can browse today is a preview, not a live shop. Every product carries an honest status, and none will open for checkout until we've actually done the work behind it: a real sample, real supplier paperwork, a real price.

What we can offer a customer, eventually, is straightforward: products we've actually checked, prices that are confirmed before you can pay them, and a real person to contact if something's wrong.
