Returns

Draft — no order has shipped yet.

This page is a template, written before the business has taken a single order. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a solicitor. It describes the intended position, not a tested process.

Consumer Contracts Regulations

UK consumers buying online generally have a legal right to cancel most orders within 14 days of receiving the goods, under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, and to a further 14 days to send the item back once they've told us they're cancelling. We intend to honour this in full once the shop is actually open — nothing here reduces that right.

Condition of returned items

Items should be returned unused and in their original packaging where possible. Exact return address and process will be published here once orders can actually be placed.

Refunds

Refunds would be issued to the original payment method once a returned item is received and checked. No refund timeline is published yet, because no payment system is live yet to refund against.

Faulty items

Nothing has shipped yet, so there is no track record on faults to describe. If an item is confirmed to be sold with checkout open and genuinely arrives faulty, the Consumer Rights Act 2015 gives UK buyers rights beyond the 14-day cancellation window — those rights aren't reduced by anything on this page.
