Standards

Supplier standards

These are non-negotiable. They protect the marketplace accounts we sell through and the buyers who receive the products.

Genuine wholesale invoices

Invoices must be issued directly and accurately in the name of A. Sharif Commerce. We do not request, accept, or work with altered, blank, or third-party invoices under any circumstances.

Clear EAN/UPC/SKU data

Every product needs correct, unambiguous identifiers so it can be listed and tracked properly on marketplace platforms.

Authentic branded goods

We only buy genuine, authorised stock. We do not deal in counterfeit, grey-market, or unauthorised goods.

Product-safety and compliance documents where applicable

For categories such as toys, health and beauty, or anything with electrical or chemical components, we need the relevant certification and safety documentation up front.

Transparent MOQ, lead times and delivery terms

We want to know minimum order quantities, realistic lead times and delivery terms before placing an order, not after.

On invoicing: we will not ask a supplier to alter, backdate, or reissue an invoice in a different name, and we won't work with a supplier who offers to do so. This applies without exception.
