Accessibility

What this site does to be usable with a keyboard, a screen reader, or at a larger zoom level — and where it may still fall short.

What's built in

  • A "skip to content" link at the top of every page.
  • Semantic landmarks (header, nav, main, footer) and a single page heading per page.
  • Full keyboard operation: all navigation, the mobile menu, the cart drawer, product variant/quantity controls, and the product-detail tabs work without a mouse, including Escape to close overlays and focus returning to the control that opened them.
  • Visible focus outlines on every interactive element (not suppressed).
  • Motion is minimal by default and fully disabled when a visitor's system is set to reduce motion.
  • Colour is not the only signal — status like "not yet available to buy" is always stated in text, not just colour or an icon.

Known limitations

  • No product photography exists yet — product images are placeholder graphics with descriptive alt text rather than real photos, which is itself disclosed on each product page.
  • This site has not undergone a formal, independent accessibility audit (such as against WCAG 2.2 AA) — the items above reflect what was built and checked during development, not a certified conformance claim.

Tell us about a problem

If you hit an accessibility barrier anywhere on this site, email a.sharifcommerce@outlook.com with the page and what happened — we'll look at it.
